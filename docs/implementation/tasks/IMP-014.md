## Scope and completion direction

IMP-014 must provide focused, durable Client Company and Client Site master-data behavior including:

- Client Management-owned JPA persistence;
- stable public UUIDs for cross-module references;
- explicit Company-to-Site ownership;
- creation and controlled lifecycle changes;
- deactivation without destructive historical deletion;
- narrow application/query contracts that do not expose internal entities, repositories, or relational IDs;
- authenticated administrative mutation boundaries;
- privacy-minimized append-only audit evidence for material mutations;
- deterministic validation and transaction behavior;
- real-MySQL migration and persistence verification.

Later modules must reference Client Company and Client Site through stable identities or explicit Client Management contracts rather than writing Client Management tables or repositories.

## Boundaries

IMP-014 does not implement:

- Manpower Request / Job Requisition lifecycle; that belongs to IMP-015;
- recruitment pipelines;
- Worker/Employment;
- Position, Deployment, Assignment Terms or compensation;
- scheduling, attendance, payroll or billing behavior;
- Client Portal identities or synchronization;
- customer-specific extensions or source forks.

Do not introduce a generic workflow engine, microservice, external cache, queue, distributed database, or additional deployable application.

Do not make Client Company or Client Site JPA entities/repositories public cross-module APIs.

## Database direction

Use JPA/Hibernate for normal Client Management persistence.

Released V1-V10 migrations are immutable. TASK-0037 and TASK-0039 require no schema change.

Do not seed customer Client Companies, Client Sites, production authorization assignments, credentials, or other installation-specific data.

Historical references must be preserved. Normal lifecycle behavior uses explicit activation/deactivation rather than destructive deletion.

## Security, privacy, and audit

Administrative mutations must derive the authenticated actor through `CurrentActor`; caller-supplied actor identifiers never determine authorization or audit attribution.

Use a narrowly named Client Management authority rather than broad implicit access. Business-module permission definitions must not be silently seeded into production authorization data.

Audit only stable non-secret identifiers and bounded action context needed to explain the mutation. Do not record arbitrary request serialization, credentials, session identifiers, infrastructure causes, or unnecessary personal data.

The initial Client master slice should avoid collecting contact-person or other personal information unless a later focused requirement explicitly needs it.
